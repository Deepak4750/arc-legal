# Delete your Arc account

This page is for the Arc app (formerly Flow), published by Arc Wellness (M. Deepak Reddy).

## If Arc is installed

1. Open Arc and go to **Settings**.
2. Tap your profile card at the top.
3. Under **Account**, tap **Delete my account**.
4. Confirm with **Delete account**. Google may ask you to pick your account first. You need an internet connection.
5. Arc then asks whether to also erase the data on your phone. Choose **Erase phone data** or **Keep it**.

## If you have uninstalled Arc

Send an email to **arc.app.admin@gmail.com** from the Google account you used with Arc, with the subject **Delete my Arc account**. We delete the account and reply within 30 days. You do not need to reinstall the app.

## What is deleted

- Your Arc account in Firebase Authentication: Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture link.
- Any streak record Arc's server holds for your account.

## What is not deleted by this, and for how long it is kept

- Bug reports you sent: they are not linked to your account. They are deleted when the bug is resolved and never kept longer than 12 months. To have one removed sooner, email us and describe it and when you sent it.
- Usage data (only if you turned it on): not linked to your account, kept for 2 months.
- Crash reports (only if you turned them on): not linked to your account, kept for 90 days.
- Data on your phone: it never reaches us. Remove it with **Erase phone data**, by clearing Arc's storage or by uninstalling. If Keep data was on, also delete the Documents/Flow folder.

Deleting your account does not delete your Google account.
